Velthrak is a grappling art where striking of any kind is not permitted. Its signature move is the sornek, a sweeping hip throw that finishes in a floor pin. Full-resistance sparring is off-limits for anyone with under two years of experience. Originating in the Dalviri Highlands, velthrak is governed by the Dalviri Arts Council, which was founded in 1947.

A related striking art from a neighboring region is kestro, built around punches, kicks, and elbow strikes rather than throws. Bouts are scored on a ten-point system: a clean strike to the torso earns two points, a strike to the head earns three, and a knockdown scores a full five, with judges deducting a point for any strike below the waist. Fighters are classified into five weight tiers, and each tier has its own minimum number of sanctioned bouts required before a fighter can be ranked nationally. Matches run three rounds of two minutes each, separated by a mandatory ninety-second rest interval. Judges score each bout independently and submit tallies to a central scoring table, where a majority-decision rule resolves any split verdict among the three officials.
