# Books — Feature Table

**Setting**: *New Meridian* by James Obi — a literary superhero novel told in alternating perspectives, set in the fictional city of New Meridian. Not a comic book adaptation; the novel treats its characters as people who happen to have abilities, and is mostly concerned with what those abilities cost them and the people around them.

**Tested concepts:** Solara, Greywall
**Distractor concepts:** Undertow, Cipher
