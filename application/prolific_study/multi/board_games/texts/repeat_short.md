Stormgate is a game of ancient conflict set across a stormy archipelago, where players command rival elemental factions locked in a struggle for dominance. That stormy archipelago is carved into seven contested gateways, each shifting allegiance as the elemental factions advance. It is played on a grid of hexagonal battle tiles, and storm tokens track the shifting elemental forces on the board. The storm tokens in particular change the battlefield from round to round, forcing factions to shift their positions. It was designed by Mira Tolenko and published by Brackenmoor Games in 2019. Tolenko brought the game to Brackenmoor Games after several years of independent development.

Veiled Court takes a different approach. Set in a Renaissance court full of scheming nobles, it puts players in the roles of aristocrats vying for influence and power. Every alliance in that Renaissance court is fragile — nobles who backed you one round may work against you the next. Influence tokens are the main currency of the game, while masked role cards keep each player's allegiance secret. Those influence tokens shift constantly as players negotiate, and a single revealed role card can upend the standings. It was designed by Cassian Rowe and published by Vellichor Publishing in 2021. Rowe brought the game to Vellichor Publishing after a self-published version gained traction at game conventions.
