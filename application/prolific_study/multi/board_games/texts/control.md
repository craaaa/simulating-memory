Stormgate drops players into an ancient conflict across a stormy archipelago, where rival elemental factions are locked in a struggle for control. The board is a grid of hexagonal battle tiles, and storm tokens mark the shifting elemental forces sweeping across it each round. Everyone locks in their moves simultaneously before anything is revealed — which means the hex you commit to holding might be exactly the one your opponent chose to attack. Two to four players can take part. Mira Tolenko designed it, and Brackenmoor Games published it in 2019.

Veiled Court is a different kind of game. Players are nobles in a Renaissance court, each working to accumulate influence while keeping their true allegiances out of sight. The masked role cards define what each player is actually trying to do; the influence tokens show how well they're managing it. All decisions get settled by secret ballot — so a group can vote in a direction it turns out to collectively regret. It seats four to eight. Cassian Rowe designed it for Vellichor Publishing, which released it in 2021.
