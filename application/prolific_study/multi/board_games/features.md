# Board Games — Feature Table

**Tested concepts:** Stormgate, Veiled Court  
**Distractor concepts:** Ironmarket, The Wandering Carnival

Feature roles: **T** = tested (in questions), **P** = padding (control/repeat only), **D** = distractor concept feature

| Feature | Role | Stormgate | Veiled Court | Ironmarket | The Wandering Carnival |
|---------|------|-----------|--------------|------------|------------------------|
| Theme / Setting | T | Ancient conflict between elemental factions on a stormy archipelago | Renaissance court intrigue; players are nobles vying for influence | Victorian-era industrial city; players are rival merchant guild leaders | A mysterious traveling carnival that appears overnight in different towns |
| Signature components | T | Storm tokens and hexagonal battle tiles | Influence tokens and masked role cards | Brass guild seals and a hand-cranked price wheel | Carved performer meeples and wonder lantern tokens |
| Designer / Publisher | T | Mira Tolenko; Brackenmoor Games (2019) | Cassian Rowe; Vellichor Publishing (2021) | Petra Halms; Bellwether Games (2018) | Noel Cartwright; Duskwatch Studios (2022) |
| Core mechanic | P | Hex-and-counter area control with simultaneous action selection | Hidden role negotiation with secret ballot voting | Commodity trading with a fluctuating price track | Push-your-luck exploration with deck-building elements |
| Player count | P | 2–4 players | 4–8 players | 3–5 players | 1–4 players |
| Game duration | D | 90–120 minutes | 45–75 minutes | 60–90 minutes | 30–60 minutes |
| Player interaction | D | Highly confrontational; players directly attack each other's territory | Deeply social; all decisions involve negotiating with and reading other players | Moderate; trading is cooperative but final scoring is competitive | Low conflict; competitive but players rarely block each other directly |
| Award / Accolade | D | Origins Award for Best Strategy Game (2020) | Golden Geek Award for Best Party Game (2022) | Nominated, Dice Tower Award for Best Mid-Weight Game (2019) | GAMES Magazine Game of the Year (2023) |
| Expansions | D | One expansion: *Tidewrath* (adds a 5th faction) | None published | Two expansions: *Rail Barons* and *Harbor Lords* | One expansion: *The Midnight Show* (adds a nightmare carnival mode) |
| Recommended age | D | 14+ | 12+ | 13+ | 10+ |
