Let me introduce you to the Ashen Skreel. It gets by on a diet of insects, supplemented with whatever small carrion it can find. In other words, it's an opportunist, taking live prey when it can and scavenging when it must. The skreel's plumage is a muted slate-gray, broken only by a pale patch at the throat. That gray coloring is so close to the rock around it that a perched Skreel can be almost impossible to pick out unless you notice its whitish throat patch.

The Copperhook Finch leads a very different life. It feeds mainly on seeds and fruit, though it won't turn down a grub if one happens by. So while it's a seed-eater at heart, it's happy enough to take a bit of animal food when the chance arises. The finch's body is a warm rust-orange, set off by sharp black wingtips. Those orange tones make it one of the brightest birds you're likely to see in a hedgerow.
