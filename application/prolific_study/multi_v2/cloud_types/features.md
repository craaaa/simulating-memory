## Foil-disambiguation notes

- **Altitude**: four distinct bands (below 800 m / 2,000–4,500 m / 4,500–7,000 m / 7,000–11,000 m) — no overlap.
- **Composition**: Flocculenth = supercooled liquid only; Veilspire = ice only; Patchmantle = large liquid droplets; Ridgewing = mixed. All four differ.
- **Weather signal**: frontal rain vs. fair weather vs. drizzle vs. mountain waves — unambiguous.
- **Color**: Flocculenth is gray-and-ivory; Veilspire is bright white/iridescent — clear contrast for Q4 2×2 foil structure.
- **Classifier**: four distinct names, nationalities, and years — no two share any attribute.
- **Cloud1 (Flocculenth Cloud)** is the tested concept for all questions. Cloud2 (Veilspire) supplies foil values.
