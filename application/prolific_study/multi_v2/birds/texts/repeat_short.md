Let me introduce you to the Copperhook Finch. Its body is a warm rust-orange, set off by sharp black wingtips. That orange-red body and those dark-tipped wings make the Copperhook Finch easy to spot. 

Although nuts aren't part of its diet, it feeds on seeds and fruit, and will happily eat a grub if one happens by. When fruit is abundant it'll gorge on little else; leaner times bring it back to seeds and whatever grubs it can find. 

Its scientific name is Fringilla rufa. Fringilla is Latin for finch, placing it among its closest relatives; rufa captures its warm reddish color.
