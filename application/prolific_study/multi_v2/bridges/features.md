# Bridges — Feature Table

**Tested concepts:** Crannock girder, Talvez arch
**Distractor concepts:** Hesswick truss, Morden cable

Feature roles: **T** = tested (in questions), **P** = padding (control/repeat only), **D** = distractor concept feature

| Feature | Role | Crannock girder | Talvez arch | Hesswick truss | Morden cable |
|---------|------|-----------------|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| Load mechanism | T | tapered cross-beams carry load to dual end pylons | parabolic stone arch redirects load to rock foundations | diagonal tension rod network carries load to abutments | fan of steel cables carries load from a single central tower |
| Primary material | T | laminated iron plate | cut limestone | wrought iron | high-tensile steel cable |
| Span range | T | 40–120 meters | 20–60 meters | 30–90 meters | 150–400 meters |

| Construction method | P | prefabricated sections bolted together on-site | keystone-wedge centering built from both banks inward | riveted rod assembly with turnbuckle tensioning | tower-first erection with cables tensioned outward from centre |
