The Frostmere Tanager (*Cyanotilla gelida*) is a high-mountain bird with a deep indigo back and a vivid rust-orange chest band. It probes alpine flowers for nectar and surface-gleans aquatic insects from pool margins. Its call is a descending two-note whistle that carries well across open terrain. The species inhabits high-altitude bogland above two thousand meters, foraging near the water's edge through the warmer months. It builds a compact cup nest of woven sedge stems, fixed to bogland vegetation just above the waterline.
